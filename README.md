
# Rachel's Cakes

This repository is the source for Rachel's Cakes' website — a small business site that showcases Rachel's handcrafted cakes and lets customers order custom cakes online. It's the reference for anyone building on, maintaining, or extending the site.

## What's in here

The site covers everything a visitor needs to browse Rachel's work and place an order. Here's what you'll find when you open the published site:

| Page | What it covers |
|------|-----------------|
| Home (`index`) | Hero section, business highlights (years baking, cakes delivered, handmade stats), and calls to action to browse or order |
| Browse Creations | Gallery of cake designs — birthday, character, tiered/wedding cakes, and more |
| About Rachel | Background on the baker and the business |
| Learn With Rachel | Hands-on baking workshops and private lessons |
| Order Now | Form for customers to submit custom cake requests |
| Contact Us | Location, phone, and email for inquiries |

Supporting assets live under the project's assets folder, including cake photography and brand imagery used throughout the site.
